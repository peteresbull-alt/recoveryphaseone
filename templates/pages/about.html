About Vaultreck

Rigorous investigation. Lawful recovery. Real discretion.

Vaultreck was founded to give fraud victims — individuals and institutions alike — access to the same calibre of financial investigation historically reserved for large corporate matters.

Our Mission

Every victim of fraud deserves a rigorous, honest investigation.

Fraud recovery is often inaccessible or exploited by bad actors preying on people who have already lost money. We exist to change that — combining investigative rigor with honest, realistic communication at every step.

Our Approach

Evidence-led, institutionally coordinated, never overpromised.

We build every case on verifiable financial and blockchain evidence, work directly with the banks, exchanges, law firms and law enforcement bodies positioned to act, and we are always honest about what is and isn't realistically achievable.

Our Values

What guides our work

Rigor

Every finding is built on verifiable evidence, not assumption.

Discretion

Every case is handled in strict confidence, with sensitivity to what clients are going through.

Lawfulness

We pursue recovery exclusively through legitimate institutional and legal channels.

Honesty

We never promise outcomes we cannot control, and we say so clearly, every time.

Compliance & Ethics

Operating within the law, always.

Vaultreck operates exclusively through lawful investigative methods and coordinates with licensed institutions, regulated exchanges, qualified legal counsel and law enforcement bodies. We do not engage in self-help recovery, unauthorised account access, or any activity outside the law in any jurisdiction in which we operate.

Full details of our regulatory registrations, licensing and professional affiliations are set out in the footer of this website and are available on request.
