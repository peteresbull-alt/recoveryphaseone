Spot a Recovery Scam

Fraud victims are often targeted a second time by people posing as recovery experts. Protect yourself by knowing what a legitimate firm will never do.

  • We never guarantee recovery. No legitimate firm can promise an outcome.
  • We never ask for upfront payment in cryptocurrency or gift cards.
  • We never contact victims unsolicited claiming to have located their funds.
